Key Takeaways Internal communication is the structured, two-way flow of information and feedback between people within an organization, distinct from external communication with customers and media. Internal communication is a critical function for organizational operations and HR professionals. Strong internal communication directly impacts company performance by keeping employees aligned with strategic goals.
