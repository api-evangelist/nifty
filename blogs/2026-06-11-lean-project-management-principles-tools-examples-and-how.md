Teams are often busy, but that doesn't always mean work is moving forward. Delays caused by unclear priorities, repeated approvals, manual status updates, and scattered communication can slow even the best projects. Lean project management helps teams deliver more value by removing the work that doesn't contribute to the final outcome.
